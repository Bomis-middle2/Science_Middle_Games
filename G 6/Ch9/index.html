THE MAGNETISM MISSION

Stage 1: The Shepherd's Lost Stick
You open your eyes and find yourself in ancient Magnesia, Greece. A worried shepherd named Magnes runs up to you.

"Help! My herding stick is stuck flat against a black mountain rock, and I can't pull it away!"

The tip of his stick is made of iron. The rock is a dark, natural mineral pulling it via natural magnetic forces.
Stage 2: The Sorting Gates
The mountain wall slides open, revealing a futuristic laboratory with two large bins:
BIN A: MAGNETIC  |  BIN B: NON-MAGNETIC

Scattered on the floor are four fallen items:
• Plastic Bottle
• Iron Nail
• Aluminium Foil
• Cobalt Coin

To cross the threshold, you must sort the magnetic items using your stick.
Stage 3: The Compass Maze
You step forward and find yourself in a thick, misty forest. You cannot see the sun or landmarks.

You take a magnetic navigation compass out of your coat pocket. You hold it flat, and the needle settles down. The red half of the pointer aligns directly toward a giant oak tree in front of you.
Stage 4: The Weakening Captain
You arrive at an island control tower. Captain Lily is distressed:
"Our emergency backup steering system uses a powerful Permanent Bar Magnet, but it has completely lost its magnetism!"

You read the crew's daily maintenance log:
  1. They wrapped it in wood and kept it in pairs with unlike poles together.
  2. They accidentally dropped it from a high balcony onto the metal deck.
  3. They wiped it clean with a wet cloth.
  4. They left it sitting far away from electronic devices.
Stage 5: The Crane Master
To escape the island entirely, you must navigate a massive scrap crane to pick up a huge shipment of iron beams and pack them into your ship. This crane uses a temporary magnet powered by electric current.

You enter the cockpit. A soft iron core is tightly wrapped with copper coils leading to a battery switch. The crane is down over the iron scrap, but nothing sticks.
